# Service runbook

## Signals and impact

Identify the service or journey, alert or symptoms, dashboard, log or trace sources, and how to establish the scope of impact.

## Diagnosis

Describe initial checks, expected signals, and the hypotheses each check distinguishes.

## Recovery and verification

Explain how to prove journey recovery, reverse unsuccessful changes, and apply escalation criteria that are actually known.

## Evidence and limitations

Record exercise dates and environments only when an exercise was actually performed. Do not invent contacts, drill results, or production commands. Remove irrelevant sections before delivering the document.
